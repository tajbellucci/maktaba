## Intent

A silent "how to use our product" demo of the Maktaba Naumania desktop app, not a
launch video. For the library staff and the madrasa administration. The complete
flow of the application: open the library, browse, search by name, Unicode
full-text search and the reader screen, add a book and save, add a cover photo,
issue and return, delete (tick then حذف کریں then confirm), automatic
publishing, theme and font (appearance), categories and backup. Modern motion
product-video style.

## Assets

- screens/*.png — real screens recorded from the running app (test copy of the
  catalogue, borrower data blank), 3200x1800 each, one per step.
- assets/JameelNooriNastaleeq.ttf — the app's own Urdu font.
- assets/logo.png — the app's logo.

## Customizations

- No music, no voice, screen only. Every caption must be readable with sound off.
- Animated cursor clicks, highlight ring on each button used, camera zooms.

## Notes

- Shown on WhatsApp and an LCD screen: one 16:9 render serves both.
- Urdu step captions, Space Mono step counters.
- The user asked for one minute, then asked to add theme, font and the reader
  screen and said go; at silent-caption reading speed that comes to ~100s.
- Never show real borrower names or the Settings screen (it shows connection keys).

- 2026-09-30: user asked where book issuance was; step 10 expanded into three steps (issue, return, issued and overdue lists) from a full captured circulation flow with a fictional borrower.
